## 7. Do’s and don’ts (white-hat guest posts)

### Do
- Pitch sites your readers would actually trust (topical blogs, niche media, university resource pages).
- Write original content; match the host’s tone and length.
- Disclose affiliation if you help run ToolVerse.
- Use 1–2 natural links; prefer contextual tool or guide URLs over stuffing five homepage links.
- Add value first (steps, screenshots, checklists); link second.
- Accept editor cuts; never demand dofollow or exact anchor text.

### Don’t
- Buy guest posts, PBNs, link farms, or “DR50 niche edit” packages.
- Cloak links, hide text, or keyword-stuff anchors.
- Spam the same article to 20 sites (duplicate content).
- Fake reviews or “ToolVerse changed my life” testimonials you didn’t earn.
- Overclaim tax/paycheck tools as official filing advice—always “estimate only.”
- Mass-comment blogs with “nice post + link.”
